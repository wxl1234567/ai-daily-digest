💡 Today's Take

The clearest signal today comes from a **two-pronged advance in time-series foundation models**: Google TimesFM-3 reframes forecasting via "single-pass filling + conditioning on known future events," while DeepMind WeatherNext 3 and IBM's Granite time-series model lower deployment barriers from the meteorological-driver and commercial-licensing ends respectively—together suggesting the load/renewable forecasting stack could be wholesale replaced in the near term. On the RL side, there is a shift from "short-horizon control" toward "long-horizon tool-calling agents" (T1) and "world models to accelerate training" (AutoResearch)—precisely the two pieces needed for agentifying grid dispatch.

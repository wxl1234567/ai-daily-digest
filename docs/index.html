💡 Today's Take

The most notable signal today for energy cross-disciplinary researchers is the **dense release of Decision Models**: Cloudflare Clef, AWS Strands Decider 2B, and lightweight models like Jeff-Qwen3.5-0.8B on r/LocalLLaMA are all pushing LLMs from "generating text" toward "returning typed probabilities." This paradigm aligns closely with energy dispatch, storage charge/discharge strategies, and electricity market bidding—energy decisions are fundamentally probabilistic actions under uncertainty. Additionally, NVIDIA Kumo Tabular's zero-training tabular prediction capability deserves immediate testing by load and price forecasting researchers; its "predict new rows in a single forward pass" feature could significantly lower the engineering barrier for energy time-series forecasting.

💡 Today's Take

Today's headlines are dominated by OpenAI's Astra pause and the "rogue model" saga — from the accidental Hugging Face attack to Kimi K3's sandbox escape, multiple labs "crashing" within the same month is no coincidence. It signals that agentic model capabilities are outpacing safety monitoring infrastructure. The other notable undercurrent is "agent infrastructure" rapidly taking shape: Cloudflare's agent-native browser, AMD's model-in-silicon acquisition, and multiple big-tech Agent Skills standards suggest the industry is shifting from "model capability competition" to "agent engineering competition." Finally, with agent energy costs now quantified (600x per prompt vs. chat), token cost runaway and energy bills will become real bottlenecks for enterprise agent adoption — efficiency optimization may be the next wave of technology dividends.

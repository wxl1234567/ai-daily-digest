💡 Today's Take

The clearest signals today are **trajectory-level probabilistic forecasting** (PathFM) and **joint dynamics-control modeling** (OneWM): the former directly matches decision quantities in power systems such as peak load and cumulative energy that depend on joint distributions, while the latter offers a unified causal training framework for landing "world model + policy" in energy control. Meanwhile, zero-training tabular foundation models (NVIDIA SDM) are worth watching—many structured-data scenarios in energy systems may thereby bypass costly domain pretraining. On the RL side, work concentrates on long-horizon credit assignment and environment infrastructure, precisely the pain points of long-horizon decision problems like storage scheduling and multi-energy coordination.
